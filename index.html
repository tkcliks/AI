🧬 1. The Intelligence Spectrum Map

Supervised ML Predictive

Trains on explicit labeled input-output pairs to predict fixed outcomes.

Business Benefit: High mathematical determinism, sub-50ms latency, and exact auditability.
Stack: Scikit-Learn, XGBoost, PyTorch
Example: Bank Transaction Fraud Scoring
Deep Learning Neural Nets

Multi-layered neural networks extracting features from unstructured media.

Business Benefit: Eliminates manual feature engineering for computer vision & speech.
Stack: PyTorch, TensorFlow, OpenCV
Example: Manufacturing Visual Defect QA
Generative AI Transformers

Autoregressive foundation models generating text, code, or images.

Business Benefit: 90% faster draft response generation & document summarization.
Stack: GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, vLLM
Example: Telecom Support Draft Automation
Agentic AI Ecosystems Autonomous Workflow Engine

Combines LLM reasoning with state machines, short/long-term memory, and dynamic tool calls (APIs, SQL databases, ERP systems).

Tech Stack: LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, FastMCP, Qdrant, LiteLLM
Enterprise Impact: 70-80% end-to-end insurance claim triage & payout containment.

🔄 2. Foundation Models, Fine-Tuning & Lifecycle Management

Navigating model adaptation strategies across pre-training, fine-tuning, alignment, and RAG.

Pre-Training (Base Models)

Broad world knowledge learned from trillions of web tokens. High cost ($10M+), provided by foundation model vendors.

Tech: Megatron-LM, AWS Bedrock
Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT / LoRA)

Adapts model weights on specialized Q&A pairs to enforce legal/medical terminology and strict JSON outputs.

Tech: Unsloth, Hugging Face TRL, Axolotl
Human Alignment (DPO / RLHF)

Aligns generation with human preference criteria to penalize toxic responses and brand-damaging hallucinations.

Tech: Alignment Handbook, Label Studio
